الشوط الخامس · The 5 as the identity

Round 3. The 5 becomes the whole brand. Each mark is drawn from his banner's world (clock, pitch, film, newsprint) and shown large, as a YouTube avatar at real size, and in a banner lockup.

1. ساعة الحكم · Referee's Watch

The bowl of the 5 is a stopwatch face, and the red hand stops on the fifth mark. It ties straight into the clock in his banner. Reads as a 5 first, as time second.

YouTube avatar

2. الملعب · Pitch Lines

A 5 drawn only in pitch markings: the corner arc and flag top-left, the touchline as the stem, the centre circle as the bowl, the centre spot in red. Monoline and premium.

YouTube avatar

3. شريط الأرشيف · Archive Reel

A 35mm film frame. The Latin 5 holds the Arabic ٥ in its bowl, so one number appears in both scripts. Fits the 'never-told stories' documentary angle.

YouTube avatar

4. طباعة الجرائد · Halftone

Old-newspaper halftone dots: an ink 5 printed over a red floodlight. Two-colour riso-print feel, the most artsy of the five, and still bold at avatar size.

YouTube avatar

5. التذكرة · The Ticket

Supporting piece

A ticket to a match that was never played. Not an avatar: an end card, merch or thumbnail stamp that any of the four marks can sit on.
