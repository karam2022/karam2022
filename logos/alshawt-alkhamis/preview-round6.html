الشوط الخامس · custom logotype

Every letter here is built by hand on a grid: one stroke weight, square Kufic joins, and rhombus dots like the cut of a qalam. No font is involved. The name works like a modern Arab media wordmark, and the word itself does the branding.

The idea: the loop of the م in الخامس is drawn as the digit ٥, in red, so "the fifth" literally holds the five. It's the only accent in the name, and it also stands alone as the avatar.

Regular: the main version

The weight for the channel banner, video intros and lower thirds.

Light: the premium version

A finer line for title cards, merch and print. It feels more editorial.

Colourways

Gold on black for the premium, documentary feel; cream on pitch green for sport; ink on paper (above) for the archive mood of his banner.

The avatar: the ٥ on its own

The red ٥ lifted straight out of the logotype. A single shape, readable at any size, and it belongs to the name.

As a system with the girih ball

The two directions combine: the logotype carries the name, and the girih ball is the emblem (avatar, watermark, merch). Arabic letters and Arabic geometry together.
