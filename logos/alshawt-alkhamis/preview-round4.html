الشوط الخامس · Symbols

Round 4: symbol-first. No letters inside the marks. Each one carries a single idea from the channel: five blades, the whistle, a look behind the game, the tunnel, the extra half.

1. العدسة · Aperture

A camera iris with exactly five blades. The opening they leave is a pentagon, the patch on a football. Five, film and football in one shape, with no letters.

2. الصافرة · The Whistle

From his own line ما وراء الصافرة (beyond the whistle): a referee's whistle mid-blow, with the pea inside drawn as a red ball. Instantly readable at any size.

3. ثقب الباب · Keyhole

The centre circle and halfway line, seen from above, become a keyhole: a look behind the game. The ball glows in the middle.

4. النفق · The Tunnel

Five arches leading out of the players' tunnel (like the photo in his banner). The fifth is the light at the end, with the ball waiting there.

5. الكسوف · Eclipse

A ball split into its two halves, with a red fifth half rising behind them like an eclipse. The red shows through the gap between the halves. The most minimal of the five.
