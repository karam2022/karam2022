الشوط الخامس · الكرة المزخرفة, the girih ball

A real football (32 panels: 12 pentagons, 20 hexagons) drawn with the traditional method behind Islamic star patterns. Lines leave every panel edge at the same angle and meet inside it. That turns each pentagon into a 5-point star and each hexagon into a 6-point star, all joined into one continuous girih.

At the centre is a five-fold rosette (the "fifth"), and the ball's own patch is red. Close up, as an avatar, it reads as an Arabic rosette; zoomed out, it's clearly a ball.

نحاس · Brass

Night match: brass strapwork on black, deep-green stars. The most premium of the three, like inlaid metalwork on a Damascus box.

زليج · Zellige

The colours of Moroccan and Andalusian tilework: cream bands, cobalt, green and ochre. Warm, crafted, unmistakably Arab.
